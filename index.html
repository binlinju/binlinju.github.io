About Me

I am currently working as an Algorithm Engineer at Alibaba Local Service Company, where my primary responsibilities encompass optimizing marketing and recommender systems through algorithmic enhancements and efficiency improvements. Additionally, I actively investigate cutting-edge AIGC methodologies to drive innovation in business applications.

I received my M.E. from the Intelligent Gaming Laboratory in the Department of Automation and Artificial Intelligence at Nanjing University (NJU), under the supervision of Professor Xianzhong Zhou. Previously, I obtained my B.E. from Northeastern University(NEU). My research interests span reinforcement learning, AIGC, advertising & marketing, and recommender systems.
